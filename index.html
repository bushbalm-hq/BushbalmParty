Good to Know

The important stuff: who you can bring, where to go and how not to get left outside the casino.

Bringing a +1?

Who counts as a +1?
A partner, a friend, a family member. Basically anyone you’d actually want to bring along for the fun.

Not invited: pets or kiddos. Sorry besties. :/
What are they invited to?
Your +1 is invited to The Big Bush Bash: Gatsby Gala only. All other Bushbalm holiday activities are employees-only.

Their spot at the Gatsby Gala is covered, but travel and any other costs outside of the Gala are on you.

And most importantly: make sure you’ve RSVP’d for your +1 so we know they’re coming. No surprise guests, divas. ♡

Hard Rock Hotel and Casino info

Finding The Joint
The Gala takes place in an event space called The Joint.

The Hard Rock is basically a maze. If you get lost, let any staff member know you’re part of the event in “The Joint”.

Not all casino and hotel staff know that Bushbalm is the event, so use the room name instead of ours.
Which entrance do I use?
Staying at the hotel? Use the hotel and casino entrance. You can’t miss it, there’s a huge red guitar statue right outside. Once you’re through security, turn right to find the hotel check-in desk.

The hotel and casino are 19+ only, but the Gala venue isn’t. If you or your +1 are under 19, arrive through The Rotunda Entrance and go straight up the grand staircase to the festivities.

Not staying at the hotel, or not getting ready there? Get dropped off at The Rotunda Entrance for an easy arrival.
Your stay perks
During your stay, you have access to all amenities. A few perks are:
  • The gym. (Included)
  • The Sound of Your Stay. (Included) Request a Fender guitar (with headphones and an amp) or a Crosley record player with a curated selection of records, delivered straight to your room.
  • Casino access. Come and go as you wish.
  • The common areas, double as a museum of iconic outfits, instruments and artifacts from music legends.
There are also lounges, a live band, restaurants and more. Anything purchased or spent outside of the Gala is on you, paid for with your own money or your per diem.
Bushbalm does not encourage gambling and is not responsible for any gambling during your stay. Please enjoy responsibly.
Planning to bring outside food or drinks (especially alcohol) up to your room? We’ve been asked to be discreet about it. That means no huge coolers and no cases of beer, please.
