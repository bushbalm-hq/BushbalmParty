Good to Know

The important stuff: what to wear, what to pack and how not to get left outside the casino.

Dress code

  • Wednesday night: Casual + comfy. Dress for the extreme sport of competitive bowling while remaining ready to be mugging for Insta at the drop of a hat. Casual. Cute. Bowlable.
  • Thursday daytime: Business casual. Look cute. Look somewhat put together. Look like you could reasonably attend a meeting. There are always pictures, so do with that information what you will. But don’t overthink it.
  • Thursday evening: Less costume party, more gala with a Great Gatsby twist. Get your dresses out, divas. Suits, sparkle, glam, drama. This is your moment. We’re going full 1920s decadence. Prohibition, but make it Bushbalm, bb. ✨

What to pack

  • Your ID. The Hard Rock is 19+, so no ID = no entry = deeply tragic.
  • Your Big Bush Bash outfit. Obviously.
  • Warm layers. It’s Ottawa. In December. Snow? Sleet? A meteorological betrayal? Who knows. Bring the coat, hat and mitts.
  • Everything you need from morning → night on Thursday all in one bag or suitcase. We’re going straight from the daytime festivities to the Hard Rock, so your essentials need to come with you. Revisit The Bag Situation on the Schedule tab if needed.
  • For no reason,a little extra spare space in your suitcase. Don’t worry about it. 🤭
